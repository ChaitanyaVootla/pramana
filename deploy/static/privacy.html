Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Pramāṇa is a small, non-commercial history learning site run by one person, Vootla Chaitanya. This page says what it keeps about you and why.

Reading without an account

You can read every page without signing in. Nothing is kept about you as a reader beyond short technical logs (errors and requests, which can include your IP address), kept for a few days to keep the site running and stop abuse. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in, Google tells the site your Google account ID, your name and your email address. The site keeps:

  • your Google account ID, so it can recognise you next time;
  • your first name, to show it in the top bar.

Your Google profile picture's link is kept only in your sign-in cookie, on your own device, so the top bar can show your picture. Your browser loads the picture from Google.

Your email address is only compared with the site owner's, to know who may edit content, and is not stored. The site never sees your Google password and asks for nothing beyond your basic profile.

A signed cookie keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. Signing out deletes it.

What you keep here

While signed in, the site keeps what you do so you can come back to it: the pages you have read, your review cards and answers, notes, canvases and page settings. Only you can see these. They are not shared or sold, and they are not used for anything except showing them back to you.

Where it lives

The site and its database run on a server in Germany (Hetzner). Database backups stay with the operator.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything kept for it deleted, open an issue at github.com/ChaitanyaVootla/pramana/issues or write to the support address shown on Google's sign-in screen. It will be done within 30 days. You can also remove the site's access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
